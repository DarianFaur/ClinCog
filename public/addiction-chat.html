Week 4 · Substance use · Conceptualization

Jordan, 25

Content note: alcohol dependence. More

This case describes alcohol dependence, including withdrawal, drinking in the morning and driving after drinking. If this is difficult material for you right now, you can pause and come back later, or speak to your instructor.

Case summary: Jordan is a 25-year-old software engineer. He refers himself after a formal performance review in which his manager noted missed deadlines, late starts and a clear drop in the quality of his work over the past six months. He works from home most days. He is articulate and friendly but plays the problem down: “Everyone in my job drinks to unwind. I just need to dial it back a little.” He rates his motivation to change at five out of ten.
