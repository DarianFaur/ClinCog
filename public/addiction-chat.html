Week 3 · Substance use · Conceptualization

Jordan, 25

Case summary: Jordan is a 25-year-old software engineer, self-referred after a formal performance review noting missed deadlines, late arrivals, and declining code quality. He minimizes concern, rating motivation to change 5/10. Drinking escalated over the past 14 months to a daily pattern — 4–5 drinks on weeknights, 10–14 on weekend days. Three failed attempts to stop in the past year, none lasting beyond five days. Reports morning tremor, sweating, and insomnia relieved by drinking; tolerance has roughly doubled. Missed four sprint deadlines, withdrawn from his climbing gym, partner has issued an ultimatum. No prior psychiatric treatment; father was a heavy drinker.

Talk to Jordan directly, as if conducting a clinical interview. Ask about pattern of use, withdrawal, functional impact, and anything relevant to a diagnostic conceptualization. Jordan can only tell you what's documented in his case — up to 10 exchanges.
