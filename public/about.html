ClinCog

ClinCog is a teaching platform for Introduction to Clinical Cognitive Science, built to walk students through the same clinical case from three angles — categorical, dimensional, and cognitive — each week of the seminar.

Conceptualization conversations are grounded strictly in each case's written file; the model in the chat cannot invent symptoms, events, or details beyond what's documented. Evaluations (ICD-11 diagnostic criteria, dimensional questionnaires, self-administered cognitive tasks) are static and identical for every student, so what's being assessed is your understanding, not the luck of a conversation.

Nothing typed into ClinCog is stored on a server. Your name and your conversations with each case live only in this browser, on this device — see the how to use page for details.
