Week 3 · Anxiety · Conceptualization

Alex, 24

Case summary: Alex is a 24-year-old undergraduate student referred by their GP after persistent difficulties with academic performance and social engagement. Alex describes intense fear, dating back to early adolescence, of being observed or judged by others — avoiding seminar participation, declining job offers requiring customer contact, and cancelling social plans. Physical symptoms (rapid heartbeat, flushed cheeks, throat tightening) occur in feared situations, followed by hours of rumination. No panic attacks outside social contexts, no substance use, no psychotic symptoms. Symptoms present for at least three years, worsened since starting university.

Talk to Alex directly, as if conducting a clinical interview. Ask about onset, triggers, avoidance patterns, and anything relevant to a diagnostic conceptualization. Alex can only tell you what's documented in their case — up to 20 exchanges.
