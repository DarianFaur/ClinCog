Week 1 · Psychosis · Conceptualization

Dennis, 25

Case summary: Dennis, a 25-year-old college student known for academic excellence, began withdrawing roughly a year ago — spending long hours alone, losing interest in things he once enjoyed, and letting his hygiene slip. His grades declined sharply and his written work became disorganized. In recent weeks his condition escalated: he has told his family that government agents planted cameras to monitor him and that they are conspiring against him, refuses food his mother prepares believing it is poisoned, has been heard responding to voices no one else can hear, and his speech shifts abruptly between unrelated topics. He was brought in after his family found him attempting to barricade himself in his room, convinced shadowy figures were coming to take him away. This is his first psychiatric contact.

Talk to Dennis directly, as if conducting a clinical interview. Ask about onset, duration, severity, and anything relevant to a diagnostic conceptualization. He can only tell you what's documented in his case — up to 20 exchanges.
