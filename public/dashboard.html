Three lenses, one case

Every case is worked twice — a conversation to build your own understanding, then three formal instruments applied to the same patient.

01 · Categorical

Structured diagnosis

A clinical interview (SCID-5) asks whether the case meets criteria for a named diagnosis — yes or no, by symptom count.

02 · Dimensional

Severity profile

Standardized questionnaires place the case on continuous severity scales, independent of any single diagnostic label.

03 · Cognitive

Self-administered task

You complete a short cognitive task yourself, and connect what that experience felt like to the case's presentation.
