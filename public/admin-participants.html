Participant data

How the platform is used, for the feasibility study: active time, messages, the steps of each case, and the answers to your questionnaires. Recorded once the notice is accepted, never what anyone wrote. Kept until you delete it here.

Week by week

Participants active each week (Monday to Sunday), with their active hours and messages.

Through the cases

How many participants reached each step of each case.

Participants

Select a row for that participant's own record.

Questionnaires

Short questionnaires, each with a Skip button, shown to people who accepted the notice on the address you choose: in a period (once per round, for example before and after), before the first interview with a patient, or after a case is finished. Write {patient} to insert the patient's name.

Questionnaire

Changes reach participants within about 20 seconds. Editing the questions of one that already has answers is allowed, but answers to a question you remove stay in the data under its old ID.

Results

Notice

Shown once, the first time someone opens a page of the app (not the landing page), with a single Accept button; from then on their use is recorded. It can be read again from “Your data” at the bottom of every page. It does not mention a study: the study shows only in the questionnaires, each of which can be skipped.

Export

CSV files, one row per record, ready for SPSS, R or Excel. Pseudonymised exports replace student numbers and visitor codes with a stable code (the same person always gets the same code).

Delete

Deletes the study data of an address: activity, sessions, messages, case steps and questionnaire answers. Questionnaires themselves stay. For one person only, open their row under Seminar participants or Demo visitors. This cannot be undone; export first if you need the data.
