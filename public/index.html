Three lenses, one patient.

The same case looks different depending on the framework you bring to it. That difference is the lesson.

  • 01 · Categorical

    Structured diagnosis

    ICD-11 diagnostic requirements, reproduced verbatim from the CDDR, ask whether the case meets a named diagnosis.

  • 02 · Dimensional

    Severity profile

    Standardized questionnaires place the case on continuous scales, independent of any single label.

  • 03 · Cognitive

    A task you run yourself

    You complete a short cognitive task, then connect that experience to the patient’s presentation.

Your work stays in this browser. Nothing is stored on a server.

No account and no server-side records. Messages you send to a simulated patient are processed by a third-party AI provider to generate the reply, so please never enter real patient information. What this guarantees, and what it doesn’t.
