ClinCog

Clinical Cognition – Conceptualization & Assessment

Work a case the way clinicians actually do.

An open teaching platform for clinical psychopathology. You interview simulated patients, then bring three formal lenses to the same case – ICD-11 criteria, dimensional questionnaires and a cognitive task – and write up what you found.

Your first name only labels your own work on this device. There is no account and nothing to sign up for.

Need help getting started?
  • 01

    Categorical

    ICD-11 diagnostic criteria, reproduced verbatim, ask whether the case meets requirements for a named diagnosis.

  • 02

    Dimensional

    Standardized questionnaires place the case on continuous severity scales, independent of any single label.

  • 03

    Cognitive

    You complete a short cognitive task yourself, and connect that experience to the case's presentation.

Progress is stored only in this browser – no account, no server-side records. Messages you send to simulated patients are processed by a third-party AI provider to generate responses; please don't enter real patient information.
