Introduction to Clinical Cognitive Science · Seminar Practicum

One case. Three lenses.

Every week presents a single clinical case. You'll work through it twice — first by talking to the patient directly to build your own understanding, then by evaluating that same case through three formal instruments: a categorical diagnosis, a dimensional profile, and a cognitive task you complete on yourself.

01 · Categorical

A structured clinical interview (SCID-5) asks whether the case meets criteria for a named diagnosis — yes or no, by symptom count.

02 · Dimensional

Standardized questionnaires place the case on continuous severity scales, independent of any single diagnostic label.

03 · Cognitive

You complete a short cognitive task yourself, and connect what that experience felt like to the case's presentation.
