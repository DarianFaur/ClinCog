Course

What students see, and when: whether the interviews are open, when each patient is released, the periods that count exchanges, and notes at the top of every page.

Interviews

Close the chat for everyone (the rest of the site stays open), say why, and choose which of the two models students may pick.

When closed, students see the message below instead of the chat box.
Models students may use
With only one on, students do not see a model switch. The models themselves are set under AI models.

Case schedule

Release the patients week by week, or close one. A closed patient's interview stops; what students already wrote and their evaluation pages stay. The dashboard shows when each one opens.

Periods

Exchanges are counted per period. When a new period starts, every student starts again from zero; the old counts stay with the old period. Outside every period the interviews are closed. A period with no end runs until the next one starts.

 

Announcements

A note at the top of every page on uvt.clincog.net, for example "Jordan opens on Monday" or "The chat is down until 14:00". Students can close it; each new one shows again.
