Clinical Vignette

Read the case carefully before administering the assessment tools.

Jordan, 25
Software engineer · Self-referred after a performance review
Presentation

Jordan is a 25-year-old software engineer. He refers himself after a formal performance review in which his manager noted missed deadlines, late starts and a clear drop in the quality of his work over the past six months. He works from home most days. He is articulate and friendly but plays the problem down: “Everyone in my job drinks to unwind. I just need to dial it back a little.” He rates his motivation to change at five out of ten.

History

Jordan began drinking at university, at 18, mostly at weekends. His drinking increased during his first job and, over the past fourteen months, has become daily. He tried to stop three times in the past year: after an argument with his partner, after a blood test showed abnormal liver results, and once on his own initiative. None of these attempts lasted more than five days, and each time he was soon drinking as much as before, or more.

Current difficulties

Jordan now drinks four or five cans of beer on weeknights, starting within an hour of finishing work, and ten or more drinks on each weekend day. He usually intends to stop after two or three but keeps going until he falls asleep or runs out. He needs about twice as much alcohol as eighteen months ago to feel relaxed. On mornings without alcohol his hands shake, he sweats and feels restless, and these symptoms ease within half an hour of his first drink; on three or four mornings a week he now drinks before his first video call. He sometimes cannot remember the end of an evening and often wakes feeling ashamed. During the day he finds it hard to sit through long meetings and thinks about his first drink for much of the afternoon.

Daily life and relationships

Jordan has missed four deadlines in the past three months and has received a written warning. He has stopped going to the climbing club he attended three times a week and has turned down several invitations from colleagues. His partner of three years has told him she will leave if nothing changes, and she now sleeps in the spare room. On several occasions Jordan has driven to the shop to buy more alcohol after drinking, saying it was “only five minutes down the road”. He has had no accidents or injuries so far.

Background

Jordan has had no previous psychiatric treatment. He says his mood is fine when he is not drinking, but he feels irritable, sleeps badly and struggles to concentrate on days when he drinks less. He does not use other drugs. His father drank heavily but was never assessed. After the abnormal liver test his general practitioner advised him to cut down. Jordan knows that the results are probably related to his drinking but has not gone back since. He says he has come mainly to keep his job.
