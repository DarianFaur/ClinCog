Week 1 · Mood · Conceptualization

Darren, 34

Case summary: Darren, a 34-year-old high school English teacher, has been struggling to get out of bed most mornings for several weeks. Once known for energetic lectures, he now feels numb, has withdrawn from colleagues, stopped cooking regularly, lost weight without trying, sleeps poorly, and struggles to concentrate. He has missed grading deadlines and walked out of class mid-lesson. In the past few weeks he has increasingly thought that others would be better off without him; last week, for the first time, he briefly considered how he might act on that thought, which frightened him enough to stop but not enough to tell anyone. On one occasion he pressed his nails hard into his forearm. This is his first psychiatric contact.

Talk to Darren directly, as if conducting a clinical interview. Ask about onset, duration, severity, and anything relevant to a diagnostic conceptualization. He can only tell you what's documented in his case — up to 10 exchanges.
