Case summary: Darren is a 34-year-old high school English teacher. His general practitioner refers him after two weeks of sick leave. This is his first contact with mental health services. He arrives on time, neatly but carelessly dressed, and speaks slowly and quietly, often pausing before he answers. Asked how he is, he says: “What’s the point? Nothing I do matters.”
