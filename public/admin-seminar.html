Seminar settings

Everything about uvt.clincog.net that used to need a terminal: who can sign in, how many exchanges each student gets, when the counters start again from zero, and the class password. Changes reach the students within about 20 seconds.

Interviews

Close the chat for everyone (the rest of the site stays open), say why, and choose which of the two models students may pick.

When closed, students see the message below instead of the chat box.
Models students may use
With only one on, students do not see a model switch. The models themselves are set under Model provider.

Model provider

Which company plays the patients for your students. Switching takes effect for the next message anyone sends; conversations carry on where they were. Try a key before switching to it.

A key saved here is used instead of the one stored in Cloudflare, and is never shown again after saving - only its last four characters. Use a paid (billing-enabled) key: on free tiers some providers may use conversations to improve their products, which is not what the Help page promises students.

Periods

Exchanges are counted per period. When a new period starts, every student starts again from zero; the old counts stay with the old period. Outside every period the interviews are closed. A period with no end runs until the next one starts.

 

Limits

How many exchanges (one question and one answer) a student gets for each patient in a period. A student's own settings, below, override these.

Students

Only the student numbers on this list can sign in. Select a row to change one student's limits, pause them, or reset what they have used.
